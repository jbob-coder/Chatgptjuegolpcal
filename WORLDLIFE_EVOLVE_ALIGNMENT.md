## How EVOLVE applies to WorldLife

1. **Current explicit user instruction** — governs desired changes, newly chosen tradeoffs, constraints, and corrections.
2. **Latest checksum-verified WorldLife Drive source** — governs factual current repository/project state.
3. **Human-readable project authority files** — README, handoff, architecture, world/interior plans, project state, tests, verification evidence.
4. **GitHub WorldLife mirror/reference branches** — navigation/history convenience after Drive verification.
5. **Derived summaries, indexes, AI memory, and conversational recollection** — acceleration/convenience only; re-verify when correctness matters.
