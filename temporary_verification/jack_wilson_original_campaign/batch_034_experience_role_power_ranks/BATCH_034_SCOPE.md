# BATCH 034 — EXPERIENCE, ROLE, AND POWER RANKS

STATUS: CANDIDATE_VALIDATED

User directive: retire universal levels and replace them with experience-based ranks; allow lower-stat but more efficient/experienced NPCs to rank higher in their profession; give healers an independent ranking; maintain a clear power record for introductions and comparisons.

Guardrails:
- No save mutation.
- No world-time advance.
- No invented NPC promotions.
- Existing Level 1 is preserved only as retired migration metadata; it is not converted into earned experience.
- Rank never grants physical stats.
- Role rank and Power Rank remain separate.
