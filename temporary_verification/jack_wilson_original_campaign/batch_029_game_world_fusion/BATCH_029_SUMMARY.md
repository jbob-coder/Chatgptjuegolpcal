# Batch 029 Summary — Crystal Era Game-World Fusion

This batch corrects pre-epoch dating so BC means Before Crystal and CE means Crystal Era with no year zero. It converts the current world into a deliberate game-version fusion: Earth remains the safest/wealthiest human homeworld by aggregate resident wealth and infrastructure security; crystal ores are mined and industrially cultivated on Earth and portal-linked worlds; beast crystals remain biological specialty resources; and offworld shelter risk creates a strong migration/wealth gradient.

The former source rival-species function is originalised as the Kharvori. The game is currently in a fragile armed peace under the 2660 CE Halcyon Gate Concord, ten years after the First Interworld War. Earth retains layered invasion monitoring and civil-defense infrastructure.

Source-transformed world-state facts through chapter 310 were used only as structural evidence. Source proper names, dialogue, protagonist plot and vampire-specific material are not imported into active game canon.
