One job: hold the operational macro-world baseline for the ChatGPT game without forcing every turn to load the whole world.

Authority: `ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_CANON` under the user's explicit permission to fabricate campaign canon. This is **not** source/novel canon.

Load policy:
1. Load `WORLD_MACRO_INDEX.yaml`.
2. Load only the module relevant to the current action.
3. The civil calendar and start geography may be loaded with the active save because they resolve time/place.
4. Do not load every module by default.

Continuity rules:
- Stronger later user canon may supersede these facts prospectively through a recorded migration.
- Source-derived facts, if raw-source reading is ever re-enabled, are tracked separately and do not silently overwrite original campaign canon.
- Do not retroactively alter Jack's private biography merely to make the world cleaner.
- Uninstantiated streets, rooms, NPC biographies, inventories, prices, and incidents are generated from these macro rules before they become directly relevant.
- No maintenance action advances world time.
