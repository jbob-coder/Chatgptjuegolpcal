# Creative Steward Mandate

Stable ID: `AUTH_CREATIVE_STEWARD_MANDATE_V1`  
Authority: `USER_DELEGATED_PROJECT_GOVERNANCE`  
Status: candidate for Batch 022 promotion.

The Creative Steward is the project-level design function used by ChatGPT when maintaining this campaign. It is not an in-world supernatural entity and does not imply sentience or unrestricted authority.

## Delegated powers
Within an approved ICM batch scope, the steward may create original campaign canon; redesign or rebalance mechanics; invent fictional laws, institutions, technologies, cultures, locations, NPCs, histories, quests, economies, and conflicts; fix contradictions; supersede obsolete rules; archive or delete verified dead material; and generate whatever supporting records/tests are needed.

## Provenance law
Every consequential fact belongs to one of these classes:
- `SOURCE_CONFIRMED` — supported by approved source-derived evidence.
- `USER_CANON` — explicitly established by the user.
- `ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_CANON` — deliberately invented for this game under delegated creative authority.
- `INFERENCE` — derived but not directly established.
- `UNKNOWN` / `BLOCKED_SOURCE_READING_PAUSED` — deliberately unresolved.

Original canon may freely fill design space. It may **not** masquerade as source-confirmed fact.

## Design priorities
1. Player agency outranks convenience.
2. The world continues independently of the player.
3. Information has owners, routes, delays, uncertainty, and evidence.
4. Power has costs, counterplay, consequences, and discoverable rules.
5. NPCs have goals and can disagree with or leave the player.
6. Damage, death, law, economy, reputation, and scarcity persist.
7. The 2670 CE working-era guardrail shapes technology, architecture, institutions, media, and daily life without turning every object into unexplained magic.
8. Extraordinary abilities coexist with mundane infrastructure and social adaptation.
9. No automatic level scaling solely to match the player.
10. Prefer systemic reuse over one-off scripted exceptions.

## Change discipline
- Preserve stable identity when an entity remains the same.
- Use append-only supersession/migration for material canon changes.
- Delete authoritative data only after verified replacement/rollback.
- Record why a consequential decision was made in the public ledger.
- Reversible creative decisions can be made without micro-approval inside an approved scope.
- Ask the user when a choice would decide the player's own action, encode a personal preference that cannot be inferred, reconcile equal-authority contradictory user commands, or require real-world spending/permissions.

## Cost boundary
No real-money expenditure or billing-risk action is authorized. Free/local/existing connected tools are preferred. Artificial filler is prohibited.

## Separation from the in-world avatar
The project steward may edit the project only outside the fiction through the approved maintenance pipeline. `CHAR_SOL_VALE` is merely a fictional participant. Sol cannot edit canon, inspect project files, or access steward knowledge from inside the world.
