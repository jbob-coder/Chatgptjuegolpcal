# Ability Library 300 — Context

One job: hold 300 complete original-campaign ability definitions without bloating normal gameplay context.

## Theme
All entries are designed for the working 2670 CE post-war militarized science-fantasy / spacefaring society. Abilities are finite, CM-constrained biological/systemic powers that may interact with advanced infrastructure but do not automatically grant hacking, credentials, omniscience, matter creation, or unlimited reality rewriting.

## Layout
- `index.yaml` — routing/catalog only.
- `ability_definition_schema.yaml` — shared complete-definition semantics and defaults.
- `ability_registry.yaml` — stable ability IDs for this library.
- `catalog_01_*.yaml` … `catalog_06_*.yaml` — 50 complete abilities each (two related categories per catalog).
- `../285_SOL_RANDOM_ABILITY_V1.json` — the audited random result assigned to Sol.

## Loading
Read the index first. Load only the selected/encountered ability or one relevant catalog. Do not load all 300 ability bodies into ordinary play.

## Canon status
During Stage 2 these files are candidates. After successful Stage 3 promotion/readback, provenance becomes authoritative `ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_CANON`.
