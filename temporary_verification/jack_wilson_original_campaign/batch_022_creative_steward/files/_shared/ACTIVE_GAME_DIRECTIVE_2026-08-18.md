## Canon creation permission
- The assistant may fabricate **original campaign canon** when useful: locations, institutions, NPCs, laws, customs, technology details, economies, abilities, quests, conflicts, histories, schedules, ecology, and mechanics.
- Fabricated material must be labeled `ORIGINAL_CAMPAIGN_CANON` (or an equivalent explicit provenance label).
- Fabricated original canon may fill an unresolved game-design gap; it must never be represented as a fact from the reference novel/source unless supported by approved evidence.
- Stronger later user canon may prospectively supersede fabricated material through a recorded migration/supersession.
- Do not use “UNKNOWN” merely because a design choice is missing when a safe original-canon decision can solve it. Preserve `UNKNOWN` only when falsely choosing would misrepresent source provenance, user identity/preference, a non-reversible player decision, or an external/current fact that requires verification.

- Prefer many small thematic YAML/Markdown/JSON files over unnecessary monoliths.

- “Unlimited/infinite creation” means create as much useful project material as practical within tool/session/storage limits; never generate artificial filler.

## Design reasoning record
- Store public decision summaries, constraints, alternatives, reversibility, and validation in the design ledger.
- Do not claim that this ledger is private chain-of-thought or hidden model reasoning.

## In-world assistant character
- A fictional assistant-controlled character may exist in the world and play alongside players.
- That character uses the same world rules, costs, injuries, information limits, social consequences, and mortality as comparable characters.
- It has no access to project files, hidden reasoning, future canon, private player information, GM controls, or author privileges from inside the world.
- The character may disagree, fail, leave, be deceived, be injured, or die according to valid game state.

## Ask the user when genuinely necessary
Ask rather than fabricate when the missing decision is primarily the user's personal preference/identity, would irreversibly choose the player's action or relationship for them, contradicts two equally authoritative user directives, or would involve real-world spending/permissions that cannot be safely inferred.
