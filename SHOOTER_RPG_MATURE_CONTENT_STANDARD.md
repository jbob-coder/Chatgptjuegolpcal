# Shooter RPG — Mature 21+ Content Standard

Status: AUTHORITATIVE CREATIVE TONE / INTERNAL 21+ TARGET
Last reconciled: 2026-09-16
Branch: `shooter-rpg`

## Purpose

Shooter RPG is being designed for an adult audience. The user selected a **21+ mature target**.

`21+` is an internal creative/content target for this project, not a claim that a storefront or ratings board has assigned an official rating. Formal ESRB/PEGI/store classification will be determined later from the actual shipped content.

## Tone

The world may be harsh, dangerous, morally complicated and emotionally consequential without becoming gratuitous for its own sake.

Allowed mature themes include:
- lethal gun violence;
- blood and visible injury;
- stronger impact consequences than a family-rated game;
- death and loss;
- criminal activity and corruption as story subjects;
- harsh language where character/context supports it;
- alcohol/tobacco or fictional intoxicants as world/story elements;
- mature relationships, betrayal, coercive institutions, exploitation, grief and trauma as narrative subjects;
- morally difficult choices without requiring a clearly "good" answer.

The mature tone should support atmosphere, stakes and character writing rather than function as constant shock content.

## Violence standard

Combat should communicate that weapons are dangerous.

Preferred direction:
- strong hit feedback;
- blood where appropriate;
- injuries can remain visible;
- deaths should not look weightless or cartoonishly consequence-free;
- environmental aftermath may show that violence occurred;
- gore can exist when justified by weapon, enemy and scene context, but should not become constant visual noise that damages readability.

Pixel styling does not require sanitizing consequences, but readability and performance still take priority over excessive effects.

## Adult relationship standard

Romance and sexual themes may exist later if they serve the game, but they are not required for the first vertical slice.

Hard project rule:
- any character participating in romantic or sexual content must be explicitly an adult and at least 21 years old in project canon;
- minors are never sexualized;
- familial/guardian relationships are never treated as sexual content;
- consent and relationship state must be represented deliberately rather than inferred from a generic score.

Mature intimacy, if later implemented, should be an authored narrative system and never an accidental result of procedural NPC state.

## Language

Dialogue may use strong profanity when it fits the speaker and situation. Do not make every character speak identically or rely on profanity as a substitute for personality.

## Substance / vice content

Adult locations or characters may include bars, drinking, smoking, gambling references, debt, black markets or other vice-oriented worldbuilding when useful to story and setting.

These are not automatically gameplay progression systems. Real-money gambling, predatory monetization and pay-to-win design are outside the foundation.

## Narrative consequences

Mature choices should have believable consequences. Examples:
- choosing survival over helping another person;
- abandoning an objective to save someone;
- cooperating with a corrupt authority for access;
- deciding whether to expose, exploit or conceal information;
- killing, sparing or arresting a dangerous adult character where the story supports alternatives.

The game should avoid fake choices whose outcomes immediately collapse back into the same state.

## First vertical slice boundary

`SHOOTER_RPG_VERTICAL_SLICE_001` does not need sexual content, romance, heavy gore or a large narrative system to prove that the game is 21+.

For the first slice, mature tone can be established through:
- grounded firearm consequences;
- environmental storytelling;
- adult dialogue tone;
- believable danger and aftermath;
- restrained blood/injury treatment if implemented;
- no childish presentation of violence.

The core control/camera/shooting quality remains the first priority.

## Content implementation rule

Mature-content intensity must remain configurable enough that later storefront/platform requirements can be addressed without rewriting gameplay architecture. Where practical, blood/gore intensity should be presentation data rather than combat-authority logic.

## Authority

This document controls Shooter RPG's audience/tone direction. It does not import mature-content rules or story material from any previous game.
